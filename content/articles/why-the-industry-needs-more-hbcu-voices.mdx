The sports industry likes to talk about representation on the field. It talks a lot less about representation in the front office, the agency, the league office, and the boardroom — the rooms where the business of sports actually gets decided.

## The gap isn't talent

It's exposure. Most students never meet someone who's done the job they're curious about, let alone someone who looks like them and went to a school like theirs.

That's the gap Behind the Game exists to close:

- Direct mentorship from working professionals in sports business
- Internship and career pathways that don't depend on who you already know
- Symposiums that put students in the same room as decision-makers

> "You can't be what you can't see." — a line every mentorship program uses, because it's still true.
