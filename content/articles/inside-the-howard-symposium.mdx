The Howard University pilot (March 2026) was the first real test of the symposium format: put students in a room with people already working in sports business, and get out of the way.

### A few early takeaways

1. Small-group format beat panel format — students asked more questions when the room felt like a conversation, not a stage.
2. The follow-up mattered as much as the event — attendees who got a direct introduction afterward were far more likely to actually use it.
3. Logistics are not an afterthought — venue, timing, and food are part of whether students show up and stay.
