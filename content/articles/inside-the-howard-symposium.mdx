On February 26, 2026, we held our first symposium at Howard University. It was a pilot, which means we came to learn as much as to host. Here's what stood out.

1. **Students came with real questions.** The best moments weren't prepared remarks. They were students asking how someone actually got their first job.
2. **Proximity changes belief.** Sitting a few feet from a professional who took a similar path makes a career feel reachable.
3. **The conversation has to continue.** A panel is a start. Students wanted contacts, follow-ups, and a way to keep going, which is why mentorship is central to what we build next.
4. **Keep it personal.** Smaller, honest conversations beat polished presentations.

## What's next

We took these lessons into our [May 2026 panel at the University of Maryland](/events), and we're building more ways to keep students connected after the event ends.
