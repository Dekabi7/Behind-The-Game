"How do I get into sports?" is the question we hear most. There's no single answer, but there are places to start.

## Start here

- **Mentorship first.** Find one person doing the job you're curious about and ask for 20 minutes, not a job.
- **Internships over prestige.** A smaller organization that gives you real reps beats a big name that gives you coffee runs.
- **Show up to the room.** Symposiums, panels, and industry nights exist because relationships still open doors that applications don't.
- **Learn the business side.** Sports is a business first. Contracts, media rights, sponsorship, and operations are where most of the jobs actually are.
- **Ask for the follow-up.** A good conversation without a next step disappears. Always leave with a name, an email, or a task.

## Where to go from here

Pick one of the five and do it this week. Message one person, sign up for one event, or read one industry newsletter. Momentum matters more than a perfect plan.
